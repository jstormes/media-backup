Print at 100% scale — do NOT "fit to page". Plain white paper, not glossy.

Method: scan in numbered order 1 → 37, pausing ~2 s between scans. If one will not read after two tries, pause and move on.

rev 2 fix: in rev 1 the two baseline barcodes were clipped by their cell and could not be read. All 37 barcodes in this revision are verified machine-decodable.
